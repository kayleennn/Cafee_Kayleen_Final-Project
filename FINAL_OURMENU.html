Coffee Latte

A coffee latte is an espresso-based drink consisting of one or two shots of espresso, a generous amount of steamed milk (usually 6 oz or more), and a thin layer of foam on top.



Matcha

Matcha is a finely ground, vibrant green powder made from specially shade-grown, stem-free tea leaves, originating from Japan.



Tea

Tea is a globally popular beverage brewed from the dried leaves of the Camellia sinensis plant, often categorized into white, green, oolong, and black based on oxidation levels.



Strawberry Shortcake

Strawberry shortcake is a classic dessert composed of tender, sweetened biscuit-like cake or sponge cake layered with macerated, sugar-sweetened strawberries and whipped cream.
