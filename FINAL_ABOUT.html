⸝⸝.ᐟ ABOUT US ⸝⸝.ᐟ⋆



Welcome to our coffee haven — a place where every cup tells a story.

Located in the heart of downtown Seattle, our café sits among the city’s vibrant streets, offering
a cozy escape from the urban rush. We believe coffee is more than just a drink; it’s an experience
that brings people together. Our journey began with a simple passion: to serve high-quality coffee
crafted with care, precision, and a deep respect for the beans and the people behind them.

We source our coffee beans from trusted farmers and sustainable producers, ensuring that every
sip supports ethical practices and exceptional quality. Each batch is carefully roasted to highlight
its unique flavor profile, whether it’s rich and bold or smooth and delicate. Our café is designed
to be a warm and inviting space where you can relax, connect, or simply enjoy a quiet moment.
Whether you’re catching up with friends, working on your next idea, or just taking a break from
your day, we’re here to make it special. Our team is made up of passionate baristas who love what
they do, from crafting the perfect espresso to creating beautiful latte art, we’re dedicated to
delivering not just great coffee, but a memorable experience every time you visit.

Thank you for being part of our story.
